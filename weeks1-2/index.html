Theory 1

Project brief and success criteria

A bedside table must be useful beside a bed, stable under everyday use and achievable with the materials, machines and time available. Before starting, turn the brief into clear checks for storage, proportion, strength, finish and safe manufacture.

1
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 2

Workshop safety and risk controls

A hazard is something with the potential to cause harm; risk considers the likelihood and consequence. Identify hazards before each task, follow the relevant SOP, use PPE and use controls that remove or reduce the hazard before relying on personal care.

2
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 3

Design planning

Sketch the overall form, drawer or shelf arrangement and hardware positions. A good design balances function, appearance, available stock and the student maker's current skill.
