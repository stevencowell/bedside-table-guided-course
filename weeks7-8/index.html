Assemble the structure

Check the frame before glue-up, then assemble it squarely and safely.

Theory 1

Dry fitting

Dry fitting reveals errors while corrections are still manageable. Check that joints close, faces are flush and both diagonals agree before glue is introduced.

1
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 2

Adhesives

Choose an adhesive suited to the timber and workshop conditions. Prepare the joint surfaces, apply enough glue for full coverage and clean squeeze-out before it cures.

2
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 3

Clamping and squareness

Use cauls or pads where needed, apply pressure evenly and recheck diagonals. More clamp pressure is not automatically better; it must hold the joint closed without distorting the frame.

3
