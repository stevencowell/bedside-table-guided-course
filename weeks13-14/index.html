Prepare the finish

Create an even, clean surface before applying a protective finish.

Theory 1

Sanding sequence

Sand progressively rather than jumping straight to a fine grit. Remove machining marks at each stage, work with the grain and clear dust before checking the surface.

1
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 2

Defect repair

Small gaps, dents or glue marks need to be dealt with before finishing. Choose a repair that matches the timber and does not create a more obvious patch after coating.

2
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 3

Inspection

Use raking light and touch to find scratches, dents and uneven edges. A good finish begins with the preparation, not the last coat.
