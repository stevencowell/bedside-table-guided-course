Theory 1

Working drawings and dimensions

Do not scale a drawing from the screen or page. Written dimensions, notes and the drawing title block control the work. Check component names, quantities and finished sizes before marking timber.

1
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 2

Cutting lists and timber selection

A cutting list translates the drawing into stock requirements. Allow material for dressing, check grain direction and defects, and keep matching parts together so the finished table looks intentional.

2
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 3

Preparing timber

Use a reliable sequence such as FEWTEL: face, edge, width, thickness, end and length. Mark a face side and face edge so every later measurement comes from the same reference.
