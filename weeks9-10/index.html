Theory 1

Storage design

A drawer or shelf must suit the intended use of the table. Decide what it stores, how much clearance is required and how it will be supported before cutting components.

1
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 2

Drawer construction

Keep drawer parts square and consistent. Check the box diagonally, make sure the base is supported and test the fit frequently instead of leaving all adjustments until the end.

2
Use it in the workshop: Record one decision or check from this part of the build in your project evidence.

Theory 3

Hardware and runners

Hardware needs accurate layout, pilot holes and a controlled fitting sequence. Test the movement repeatedly so it opens smoothly without binding, racking or excessive side play.
